# Catalog photo presentation

All 58 source photos are retained at their original URLs. Product records and admin uploads are unchanged. The storefront maps reviewed source paths to derived 1200 by 1200 WebP files using catalog-photos.js. New photo URLs without a mapping load their original through BJ_API.

41 photos received local background removal, retaining original subject pixels. Every output has at least 15% white framing around its full bounds, apart from minor codec-edge tolerance. No product detail was regenerated. Photos already clipped in the source cannot be reconstructed by framing.

17 photos retain their complete source, centered within white margins. These need manual work or replacement source photography before a safe product-only cutout is possible. The bumper sticker retains its full artwork intentionally.
