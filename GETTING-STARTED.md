# Changing the website yourself

You change the site by chatting with ChatGPT, in plain English. Nothing to install.

## Every time you want a change

1. Go to https://chatgpt.com and click **Work** at the top.
2. Start your message with the website's name, then say what you want. For example:
   - "In ZachsHacks/bitachon-judaica, make the homepage headline bigger. Open a pull request."
   - "In ZachsHacks/bitachon-judaica, give the category list on the shop page more room.
     Open a pull request."
3. When it finishes it gives you a pull request link. Click it.
4. About a minute later a comment from **Vercel** appears on that page with a **Visit Preview**
   link. That is a practice copy with your change. Check it on your phone too.
5. Happy with it? Click the green **Merge pull request** button, then **Confirm merge**.
   About a minute later it is on the real site, bitachonjudaica.com. Hold Shift and click
   reload if you do not see it yet.

Not happy? Do not merge. Tell ChatGPT what to fix in the same chat, or just close the page.
Nothing on the real site changes until you click Merge.

## Things worth knowing

- **Products, prices, and photos are not done here.** Keep using
  https://bitachonjudaica.com/admin like you already do. ChatGPT is for the design and the
  page wording: colors, sizes, spacing, where things sit.
- Regret a change after merging? Tell ChatGPT "In ZachsHacks/bitachon-judaica, undo the last
  change. Open a pull request." and merge it. Or send Zach a message.
- Stuck? Take a screenshot and send it to Zach.
