Our Story

It began on my mirror

Every morning I would wake up and write a few words where I would see them first thing: don't worry, Hashem knows what to do. Those little reminders carried me through hard mornings, and slowly they became something I wanted to share.

Bitachon means knowing that Hashem is always there. That He created us, watches our every step, and knows everything before it happens. When you have bitachon, you don't have to worry, because even the hard is good. It is a lesson Hashem wants us to learn, and He is holding our hand through all of it.

I shape every piece myself, from a lump of clay to a finished blessing for your home. No two are ever quite the same. My hope is simple: that each piece helps you remember Hashem and feel that He is with you.

Trust in Hashem.
